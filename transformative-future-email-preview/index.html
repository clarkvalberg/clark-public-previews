Dear [First Name],

Before we share the news publicly next week, I wanted you to hear it from me first: Tiferes Ventures is becoming part of Transformative.

First, the important part. Nothing changes about your existing fund or SPV positions. The same team will continue managing them under the same terms.

What is changing is the system around our investment work.

We have learned that our best investment judgment comes from doing the work, not observing it from a distance. Researching new technologies, building companies, and helping operators apply AI gives us a clearer view of which problems matter, which founders can execute, and where durable value is likely to emerge.

That work has already been happening across Tiferes and Transformative Studio. We are now bringing it together under one name: Transformative.

The model is designed to compound:

  • Research sharpens our investment theses and identifies new opportunities.
  • Studio turns the strongest of those opportunities into companies.
  • Investments backs companies we believe can become category leaders, including the Tiferes funds and SPVs you already know.
  • Advisory keeps us close to the real operating problems companies are trying to solve with AI.

Each part makes the others better. For you, that should mean better-informed investment decisions, more differentiated opportunities, and stronger support for the companies we back. You may also see selective opportunities to invest in companies formed through the Studio. Your participation beyond your existing investments is entirely optional.

The name is new. The investing relationship is not. This is the next version of the work we have been building toward together.

Our public announcement goes live later this week. Until then, I would appreciate your keeping the news confidential. I am happy to walk you through any part of it directly.

Best,ClarkManaging Partner, Transformative
